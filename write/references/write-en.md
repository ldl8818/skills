# English prose
Preserve the author's register, intent and factual precision. Prefer concrete subjects and verbs where they make the message easier to follow.
Remove redundant explanation without flattening deliberate voice. Formality follows the audience and medium.
For example, “utilize the tool to facilitate export” may become “use the tool to export” when no technical distinction is lost.
Do not manufacture anecdotes or emotional asides. Punctuation choices follow the author's or project's style, not a universal ban.
